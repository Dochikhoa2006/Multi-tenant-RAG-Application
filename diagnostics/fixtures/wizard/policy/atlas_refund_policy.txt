Atlas Refund Policy

A customer may request a refund for an accidental duplicate charge within
seven calendar days of the charge.

Credits that have already been consumed are not refundable.

Refund requests of 200 dollars or less may be processed by an authorized
billing specialist.

Refund requests greater than 200 dollars require manager approval before the
refund can be issued.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Customers should provide the transaction identifier and the reason for the
refund request.
