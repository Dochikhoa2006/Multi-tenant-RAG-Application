Atlas Production Deployment Policy

Production deployments must have a rollback plan before release.

The rollback checklist must identify the previous stable release and confirm
that a usable backup exists.

If a deployment causes a critical service failure, the operations team must
stop further rollout and evaluate whether rollback is required.

Rollback of a critical production deployment requires manager approval.

After rollback, the operations team must verify service health and document the
incident outcome.

A failed production change must not be silently retried without reviewing the
cause of the failure.
