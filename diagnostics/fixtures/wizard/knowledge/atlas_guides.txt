Atlas Support Platform Product Guide

Atlas is a customer-support platform used by Acme Corporation.

Customers can contact Atlas support through the web portal or the mobile
application.

The support assistant is available 24 hours per day, including weekends.

Standard account verification normally takes two business days after all
required identity documents have been submitted.

A verified customer can view support tickets, account status, and billing
history from the customer portal.

If a customer reports a technical problem, the support assistant first gathers
the account identifier, a description of the problem, and relevant error
messages.

Critical service incidents are escalated to the operations team.

The operations team maintains a rollback checklist for production deployments.
The checklist includes verifying the previous stable release, confirming that a
backup is available, restoring the stable release, checking service health, and
recording the incident outcome.
