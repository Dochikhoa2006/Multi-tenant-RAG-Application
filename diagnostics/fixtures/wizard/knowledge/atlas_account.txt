Atlas Account and Service Information

To create an Atlas account, a customer provides a name, email address, and
organization name.

Identity verification may require an identity document when additional account
verification is requested.

Normal verification takes up to two business days after all required documents
are received.

Customers can review active and resolved support tickets in the customer
portal.

Priority incidents are handled before ordinary support requests.

The support assistant can answer common questions automatically, but cases
requiring authorization or manual investigation are escalated to a human
support specialist.
